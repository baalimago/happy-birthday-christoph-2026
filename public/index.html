The Philosopher's Voyage

Happy birthday, Christoph. This is a sailing game, and the boat is yours.

  1. Where you are: at anchor in the home harbour, on the south coast, bow pointing at the first lighthouse, on the headland just east of you. The red anchor on the map (M) is this spot.
  2. The goal: seven places are hidden across the sea, six lighthouses and a sea cave. Sail up close to one and it is found. The Captain's orders, top left, say what to look for next; the first is that lighthouse dead ahead, and the orders talk you through sailing to it. Every place is a red cross on the map.
  3. Steer with ← and →. The compass is top right: its top is the way you are looking, and the small hull on it is the way she heads.
  4. Sails: ↑ lets them out, ↓ hauls them in. Trim until the boom meets the marker on the wind dial; the instruments tell you which way. You cannot sail straight into the wind.
  5. Easier: E starts the engine, and it works in any wind.
  6. To stop: X drops the anchor and holds her where she lies. X again weighs it.
  7. Look around: drag the sea. C switches the camera between follow (it swings back behind the boat) and free (it stays where you leave it).
  8. Each place found gives a passage for the ship's library (L), and some unlock a new view of the sea: switch with V or the buttons under the Captain's orders. Q mutes the sound. Keep off the rocks.

A Chart of the Philosopher's Sea

North is up. A red cross marks each of the seven places, ringed once found. Your own position is for you to work out, and the boat sails on while you look.

Places on the chart

The mainland, Österreich am Meer, fills the south, with the home harbour in a bay at the middle of its coast. Seaward of it, from south to north:

  • In the south: Lifetime Atoll in the west, Ferris Island, Cargo Cay, and Crate Island in the east.
  • Further out: Juniper Isle in the west, Borrow Checker Bank (a reef), QuickJS Reef and Unsafe Rocks (a reef) in the east.
  • Across the middle: Query Rock in the far west, Tokio Shoals (a reef), Wasm Holm, Mount Panic (a volcano) at the centre, Trait Holm in the far east.
  • North of the middle: Edge Harbour and Deadlock Ledge (a reef) in the west, Schattenfels (a rock with cliffs), Async Isle, Unwrap Rocks (a reef) in the east.
  • In the north: Macro Island, Segfault Shallows (a reef), Null Skerries (a reef) and Dioxus Bay in the east.
  • Along the northern edge: Melange Island in the west, Geburtstagsstrand, Closure Cay and Veltliner Holm in the east.
