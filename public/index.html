The Philosopher's Voyage

Happy birthday, Christoph. Your boat lies at anchor in the home harbour. Seven places are hidden across this sea: find them.

  1. Weigh anchor to get going: press the button below, or Enter.
  2. Steer with ← and →.
  3. Trim the sails with ↑ (out) and ↓ (in) until the boom meets the marker on the wind dial. The instruments say which way.
  4. Not moving? E starts the engine.
  5. M is the map, R the ship's library, Q the sound. Keep off the rocks.

A Chart of the Philosopher's Sea

North is up. Only the home harbour is marked: find the rest by compass and coastline.

Places on the chart

The mainland, Österreich am Meer, fills the south, with the home harbour in a bay at the middle of its coast. Seaward of it, from south to north:

  • In the south: Lifetime Atoll in the west, Ferris Island, Cargo Cay, and Crate Island in the east.
  • Further out: Juniper Isle in the west, Borrow Checker Bank (a reef), QuickJS Reef and Unsafe Rocks (a reef) in the east.
  • Across the middle: Query Rock in the far west, Tokio Shoals (a reef), Wasm Holm, Mount Panic (a volcano) at the centre, Trait Holm in the far east.
  • North of the middle: Edge Harbour and Deadlock Ledge (a reef) in the west, Schattenfels (a rock with cliffs), Async Isle, Unwrap Rocks (a reef) in the east.
  • In the north: Macro Island, Segfault Shallows (a reef), Null Skerries (a reef) and Dioxus Bay in the east.
  • Along the northern edge: Melange Island in the west, Geburtstagsstrand, Closure Cay and Veltliner Holm in the east.
