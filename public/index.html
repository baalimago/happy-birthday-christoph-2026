For Captain theduke, 11 November

The Philosopher's Voyage

Happy birthday, Christoph. Five of your projects have turned into islands, and there is a sixth you have not seen yet.

Steer with the arrow keys or the helm on screen and find the six lit ports. Each one pays out: a thought, a glass, a cup and a plate.

No WebGL aboard? Then this is the logbook, and it has everything the ports would have paid out.

  1. Home port, Vienna

    Port Wittgenstein

    The limits of my language mean the limits of my world.

    Ludwig Wittgenstein, Tractatus 5.6

    This port unlocks the Gold view: Vienna, 1900.

    Glass
    Grüner Veltliner Smaragd from the Wachau: white pepper and stone fruit. Serve it cool, not cold.
    Cup
    Wiener Melange. The glass of water beside it is part of the order; drink that too.
    Plate
    Tafelspitz with apple horseradish. Take the broth first, as soup.

  2. After juniper, your GraphQL library for Rust, 2016

    Juniper Isle

    All life is problem solving.

    Karl Popper

    This port unlocks the Chart view: the sea as a pilot would draw it.

    Glass
    A dry Riesling from the Kamptal. Or, given the island, a gin and tonic.
    Cup
    Verlängerter: an espresso lengthened with hot water, for problems that need a second pass.
    Plate
    Wiener Schnitzel with parsley potatoes. Veal, a wedge of lemon, no sauce.

  3. After quick-js, 2019

    QuickJS Reef

    Everything flows.

    Heraclitus

    This port unlocks the Terminal view: the sea, as seen over SSH.

    Glass
    Wiener Gemischter Satz: several grape varieties from one vineyard, picked and pressed together.
    Cup
    Einspänner: a double espresso in a glass, under whipped cream. Do not stir.
    Plate
    Backhendl with potato salad. Fried chicken, the Viennese way; use the lemon.

  4. After Wasmer Edge, 2023

    Edge Harbour

    If one does not know to which port one is sailing, no wind is favourable.

    Seneca
    Glass
    Blaufränkisch from Burgenland: dark berries and a little spice. It likes an hour of air.
    Cup
    Kleiner Brauner: a single espresso with a dash of cream. Small, fast and close to the user.
    Plate
    Kaiserschmarrn with Zwetschkenröster. A dessert that passes for lunch.
